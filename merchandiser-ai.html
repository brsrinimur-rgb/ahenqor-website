How It Works

From demand signal to approved purchase order

A structured buying workflow built for merchandising teams — not another forecast nobody trusts.

Signal Intake

Sales, stock, incoming inventory, ageing, sell-through, velocity, seasonality, margin, promotions and returns.

AI Recommendation

Demand forecast, dynamic safety stock and lead-time variability produce a recommended qty per SKU/store.

Transfer Before Buy

Network stock is checked first — transfers are recommended before any new purchase.

Purchase Decision

Requested vs. Recommended shown side by side for the buyer to review.

Approval

Approve, adjust or escalate against OTB budget and category thresholds.

Replenish & Monitor

Approved POs flow to replenishment; performance is tracked against the plan.

Buying Intelligence

Demand and supply intelligence behind every recommendation

Every recommended quantity accounts for more than last month's sales.

📈

SKU / Store Demand Forecasting

Forecasts generated at SKU and store level, not just category or region averages.

🛡️

Dynamic Safety Stock

Safety stock adjusts to demand volatility and service-level targets, not a fixed buffer.

⏱️

Lead-Time Variability

Recommendations account for how consistent — or inconsistent — a supplier's lead time actually is.

🎯

Service-Level Targets

Buying is tuned to the service level each category or store is expected to hit.

🏷️

Promotion Uplift

Planned promotions are factored into demand so buying doesn't under- or over-shoot.

🔍

Lost-Sales / OOS Correction

Out-of-stock periods are corrected for so historical demand isn't understated.
