Guest Journey

One folio, from reservation to check-out

Connected hotel workflows keep guest charges and operational activity tied to the same controlled guest journey.

Reservation

Booking captured across channels; room type and rate confirmed.

Advance Receipt

Deposit or advance payment recorded against the reservation.

Check-In

Room assigned, ID verified, folio opened.

Room Service / Stay

Restaurant, housekeeping and service charges can be posted to the guest folio as services are recorded.

Folio

Running guest account tracked across every department.

Invoice

Final folio reviewed and invoiced at checkout.

Check-Out

Room released, housekeeping notified, payment settled.

Operations

Every department, one system

Seven connected modules cover the property end to end.

🛎️

Front Office & Reservations

Bookings, room assignment, rate management, check-in/check-out and folio tracking across channels.

🍽️

Restaurant POS & Kitchen

Order-taking, kitchen order tickets (KOTs), live order tracking, and folio posting for restaurant and in-room charges.

🧹

Housekeeping

Room status, cleaning schedules, inspection sign-off and maintenance requests tied to room readiness.

📦

Inventory & Purchasing

Stock levels, purchase requests, GRNs (goods receipt notes) and supplier invoices across F&B and operations.

💳

Finance & Accounting

Payments, invoices, approvals and the controlled path from daily transactions through to close.

📊

Executive Dashboard

Occupancy, ADR, revenue, cash and operational exceptions in one property-wide view.

🤖

AI Analytics

Pattern detection across departments — recurring delays, stock risk and revenue anomalies.

The day doesn't close until it's verified
